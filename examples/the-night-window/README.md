# The Night Window — original short demonstration

A repair-kiosk owner has until midnight to choose between her lease deposit and correcting the battery record that could keep a courier cooperative from paying for her mistake.

This is an actual end-to-end **host-agent** writing demonstration using the Story Factory procedures: development, complete pages, self-review, revision, packaging, compilation and visual checks. It is **not** a native Claude execution test or evidence of a feature-quality guarantee. All characters, events, businesses and contract terms are invented.

No outside professional review is claimed.

## Rebuild

From this directory run `make -B all` with local Tectonic or latexmk/XeLaTeX. Fonts resolve from the repository's `fonts/` directory through `../../../fonts/` in typography settings. Make writes into ignored `output/`; curated PDFs live in `pdfs/`. A rebuild does not silently update the published copies: inspect the new output, measure it with Poppler `pdfinfo`, then deliberately replace the corresponding `pdfs/` files. [Verification](development/verification.md) and [PDF inventory](development/pdf_inventory.json) record the actual relocated build. No runtime workflow completion receipts or operational authoring scripts are published here.

## Limits

The fictional commercial rules are compressed and may feel contrived. Runtime is estimated at 8–12 minutes, not timed or filmed. The ending's inventory correction may feel overly neat. Live reading, prop/camera tests and production/legal/financial work remain undone. There are no attachments, financing guarantees, selections, awards, endorsements or delivery promises. This small demonstration establishes inspectable short-form artifacts and a working build, not production readiness or reliable feature-scale creative quality.
