% S01
\scene{INT. NIGHT REPAIR KIOSK -- NIGHT}
\action{A row of screwdrivers stands in a toothbrush cup. MAE, 43, a woman in an immaculate, repeatedly mended work coat, straightens the shortest one.}
\action{The clock reads 11:42. Mae counts cash: one hundred and eighty. She puts it in an envelope marked LEASE RENEWAL. Her terminal displays a renewal payment due at midnight. Below it: PAY TO RENEW.}
\action{She presses CONFIRM. A squeak from the window. INEZ, 31, a woman in a reflective delivery vest, pushes a handheld scanner through the opening.}
\character{INEZ}
\dialogue{It gave up at quarter past.}
\character{MAE}
\dialogue{Battery or screen?}
\character{INEZ}
\dialogue{If I knew, this would be a social visit.}
\action{Mae places it on a clean square of felt. Inez keeps her fingers on its strap.}
\character{MAE}
\dialogue{Diagnostic is twelve.}
\character{INEZ}
\dialogue{You put the battery in.}
\character{MAE}
\dialogue{That doesn't make everything it does my idea.}
\action{Inez feeds a receipt through the slot. The scanner sale is dated three weeks ago. BATTERY: NEW. 180. Mae stops straightening things.}
% S02
\scene{INT. NIGHT REPAIR KIOSK -- CONTINUOUS}
\action{Inez turns her phone toward Mae. A route roster: INEZ / SAL / DEV. Below: MORNING CONTRACT -- CONFIRM WITH ACTIVE SCANNER BEFORE 00:00.}
\character{INEZ}
\dialogue{I bought this so three of us could take the morning route. The warehouse won't confirm on a phone.}
\character{MAE}
\dialogue{Twenty years of phones. Still not a phone.}
\character{INEZ}
\dialogue{You have eighteen minutes to make that less interesting.}
\action{Mae points at the strap. Inez releases it. Mae loosens four screws, placing each in its own numbered recess. Inez reaches for a stool outside the window. Its chain catches.}
\character{MAE}
\dialogue{Lift, then turn.}
\action{Inez does. The stool settles. Mae tests the scanner at a bench supply. It lights, then dies as she removes the cable.}
\character{MAE}
\dialogue{Battery.}
\character{INEZ}
\dialogue{We could have saved twelve.}
\action{Mae opens the case. Under the battery's neat white stock label, a corner is brown. She presses it flat with her thumb.}
