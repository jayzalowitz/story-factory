# The Night Window
Original contemporary short, intended 8–12 dramatic pages. Two named women with independent professional and financial goals meet at a night repair kiosk. Understated dry humor; an ethical choice must cost something tangible. Maximum three locations; selected two. Audience: adult short-film viewers interested in work, competence and compromised solidarity. No real people/events and no borrowed plots. Complete local package requested as a workflow demonstration, not a claim of feature quality.
Assumptions delegated: fictional unnamed city; Mae owns the kiosk and needs lease renewal; Inez owns a small delivery cooperative and needs a paid route confirmation. Their conflict concerns repair records and financial responsibility. No actor/company targets or finance numbers need unsupported factual claims. End with accountability without rescue. Bechdel assessed from whole draft; professional identities and women explicitly established.
