\scene{INT. NIGHT REPAIR KIOSK -- NIGHT}
\action{11:47. Mae applies a magnifier to the battery seam. Inez watches her thumb instead.}
\character{INEZ}
\dialogue{What's underneath?}
\character{MAE}
\dialogue{Adhesive.}
\character{INEZ}
\dialogue{You've looked at it three times.}
\action{Mae lifts the label. Embossed into the metal: R17. She sets the battery down behind the magnifier.}
\character{INEZ}
\dialogue{Is R a good letter?}
\character{MAE}
\dialogue{Refurbished batch.}
\character{INEZ}
\dialogue{My receipt has a different word.}
\action{Mae opens a ledger beneath the bench. Inez hears its pages. Mae finds R17, then closes it with one finger between the leaves.}
\character{MAE}
\dialogue{Sometimes the supplier--}
\action{She doesn't finish. Inez pulls the scanner strap back toward herself. The open case catches against the window frame. Both women catch it before it falls. For a moment they hold the same fragile object.}
\character{MAE}
\dialogue{Keep it level.}
\character{INEZ}
\dialogue{I'm doing half.}
\action{Mae lowers her end. Inez lowers hers. Mae tears a blank strip from the receipt roll. She lays it over R17 and rubs a pencil sideways. The letters emerge. She hands the rubbing to Inez.}
\character{INEZ}
\dialogue{Can you put a new one in?}
\character{MAE}
\dialogue{Not until morning.}
\character{INEZ}
\dialogue{Morning is what I'm trying to buy.}
\action{Mae reaches for a drawer of batteries. Different sizes. She tries none of them.}
\character{MAE}
\dialogue{These won't fit.}
\character{INEZ}
\dialogue{I appreciate the tour.}
\action{Mae shuts the drawer. Inez smooths the serial rubbing beside her sale receipt. Two records that cannot both be right.}
