# Canon / short-film scope
Title promises a transaction viewed through a small opening that becomes a place of accountability. Logline: Before midnight, a repair-kiosk owner must choose between her lease deposit and correcting a battery record that could save a courier cooperative from paying for her mistake.
Dramatic question: will Mae put her own name and money behind a correction before Inez's route deadline? Mae, 43, a woman and sole kiosk proprietor, wants renewal, hides behind immaculate procedure, and first offers a private patch. Inez, 31, a woman and cooperative route coordinator/driver, needs a paid morning route, carries the contractual right to dispute a part serial, and initially offers a false accidental-damage claim. Both resist becoming the sole payer. Mae can certify and return money; Inez can expose the sale and refuse an incorrect signature. No other speaking roles.
Time: continuous late night; strict observable behavior; no flashbacks or magical digital repairs. Two locations: repair kiosk interior and adjoining pavement. Fictional contract explicitly printed on prop, not a legal claim. Tensions: who can afford an accurate record; whether solidarity requires surrendering leverage; whether a business can survive its owner admitting responsibility.
Ending: Mae signs a correction, refunds the purchase price from her renewal envelope, and cancels her automatic renewal payment. Inez uploads the correction after missing the route cutoff; dispute receipt promises review only. Neither livelihood is rescued. Inez buys a cheap cable, leaving an ordinary paid job for Mae. Specificity rests on the serial under a paper stock label, the refund/lease collision, and the narrow receipt slot.
