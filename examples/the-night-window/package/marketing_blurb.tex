\section{Marketing description}
A late customer. A dead scanner. A receipt with the wrong word on it. Before midnight, two working women must decide which version of a transaction they can afford to put in writing.

The Night Window is an original contained short about the price of an accurate record. Inside a tiny repair kiosk, neat screws and neat excuses stop fitting together. Dry humor, practical competence and an uncomfortably ordinary refund lead to a choice that repairs neither woman's whole life.
