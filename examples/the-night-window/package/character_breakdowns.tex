\section{Characters}
\textbf{Mae, 43, woman.} Sole proprietor, precise hands, imprecise ethics. Wants lease renewal and access to parts. Her leverage is the original ledger and her signature; her defense is classifying a moral request as a form problem. She concealed a substitution she meant to repair later. S06 forces a waiver decision; S07 turns admission into a cash consequence. Play competence and interrupted habits rather than a confession speech.

\textbf{Inez, 31, woman.} Courier-cooperative coordinator and driver. Wants tomorrow's route and to remain credible to two coworkers. Her leverage is a receipt and the option to report. She offers her own false claim before refusing Mae's waiver. She loses the booking, keeps the review alive and pays for a separate item. Her arc is from finding any workable story to insisting on a record she can stand behind. No other speaking characters; Sal and Dev exist only as roster names, with no gender specified.
