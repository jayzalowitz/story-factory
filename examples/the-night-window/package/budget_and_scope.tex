\section{Budget and scope assumptions}
This is an unbudgeted 8–12 minute short, not a financing offer. No numerical budget or recoupment promise. Two speaking performers; kiosk interior and contiguous pavement; no crowds, vehicles in motion, stunts, historical dress or functioning hazardous battery. Night scheduling, location control and readable practical displays are the main cost drivers.

Budget separately for paid prep/rehearsal, production, set dressing, safe duplicate props, sound, editing, grade, music if used, captions, accessibility, insurance, permits and contingency. A controlled kiosk build may reduce street disruption but add art costs; obtain quotes rather than assume. The refund and cable amounts are fictional story currency, not production estimates. Shoot-day count remains open pending blocking and location inspection.
