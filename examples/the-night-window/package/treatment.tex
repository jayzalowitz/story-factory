\section{Complete short treatment}
At 11:42, Mae counts one hundred and eighty into a lease-renewal envelope and schedules the midnight payment. Inez arrives at the narrow window with a scanner that will not hold power. Mae asks for a diagnostic fee. Inez produces the receipt for the battery Mae sold as new three weeks ago and the cooperative's route deadline. Mae opens the case and arranges its screws precisely.

A bench supply wakes the scanner; disconnecting kills it. Mae finds an embossed refurbished serial beneath a paper label. She tries to blame the supplier, but her own ledger records refurbished stock sold as new. She makes a pencil rubbing of the serial for Inez. No replacement tonight will fit.

Inez shows a contractual dispute process requiring a seller correction; review promises no payment. She also offers to claim she dropped the scanner under equipment cover. Mae points out the later cost; Inez closes the false claim. Mae proposes a free loan scanner tomorrow. Inez goes outside rather than accept a solution after her deadline.

On the pavement, Inez warns her coworkers not to set alarms. Mae follows with a refund pad. Signing a correction could freeze her supplier account. Inez's coworkers have committed a van and cool boxes; she bought the scanner. Mae brings Inez inside to write on a proper surface.

The refund form says full and final settlement. Inez crosses it out and writes purchase price only. Mae initially offers immediate cash without the correction. Inez insists on preserving the possibility of claiming the lost booking. Mae initials the change and exposes only the relevant ledger row for a photo, protecting other customers' details.

Mae empties her lease envelope, cancels renewal and removes her renewal card. She signs an unqualified correction. Glare spoils Inez's first photo; the second works. The route window closes. Inez submits the correction anyway and counts the refund while the upload turns. Mae reassembles the case, briefly fumbling the screw order under pressure.

At 12:02 the dispute is merely received. Inez tells her coworkers the route is lost. She pays eight for a cable. Mae tests it and identifies it as opened. Inez clears the stool chain at the door. Mae strips another false label and writes refurbished. There is no rescued lease or restored route.
