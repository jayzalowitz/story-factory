\section{Awards considerations for a short}
No award outcome, qualification or eligibility has been researched or promised. A submission campaign is premature before a filmed cut, accurate credits, clear rights and a screened audience response. Demonstrable opportunities on the page are shared physical performance in S03, status negotiation in S06, and restrained sound/prop storytelling in S07–S08.

If completed, first ask whether an awards campaign serves this short's actual audience and available resources. Verify the exact current rules of any chosen body, fees, qualifying routes and deadlines then. Do not divert production funds on the assumption that selection creates qualification or that qualification creates a nomination. This module is proportionate planning, not a prestige forecast.
