\section{Tagline directions}
\textbf{Transaction:} The receipt is correct. The word isn't.

\textbf{Time pressure:} Before midnight, put it in writing.

\textbf{Relationship:} Two businesses. One bad battery.

\textbf{Ethical cost:} Accuracy has a price.

Each direction describes the existing short. Avoid implying a crime thriller, platform conspiracy or guaranteed victory. A possible poster image is two hands holding opposite ends of an open scanner across a window; no key art exists yet. Typography should preserve the small, ordinary scale rather than advertise a prestige event or quote invented acclaim.
