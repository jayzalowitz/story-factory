\section{Logline and synopses}
\textbf{Logline:} \filmlogline

\textbf{Short synopsis:} Mae's last customer needs the scanner Mae repaired to confirm a morning delivery route. Under the battery's label is a refurbished serial; the receipt says new. Inez needs a correction, Mae needs the same money for her kiosk renewal. Neither can afford the accurate version.

\textbf{Full synopsis:} Before midnight, Inez brings a dead scanner to Mae's kiosk. Testing proves the battery failed; a serial rubbing exposes the part's actual condition. Mae's ledger proves her own substitution. Inez considers a false accidental-damage claim, then closes it. Mae proposes tomorrow's loaner, too late for the morning contract. Outside, Inez's cooperative roster reveals two other workers depending on her promise. Back inside, Mae offers a refund with a waiver; Inez refuses the waiver. Mae initials its removal, signs a seller correction and refunds the purchase price from her lease envelope. She cancels renewal to prevent an unfunded payment. Inez misses route confirmation and uploads the correction for review, without a promised payout. She buys an eight-unit cable; Mae records its opened condition, then relabels the next refurbished battery honestly. Their transaction ends; their livelihoods remain uncertain.
