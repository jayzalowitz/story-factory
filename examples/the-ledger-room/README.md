# The Ledger Room

During a forty-eight-hour records-depot closure, an archivist who finds an altered housing compensation list must enlist a courier dependent on her clean clearance to preserve evidence that implicates her own career.

This is an original AI-authored feature demonstration, with invented people, places, institutions and civic procedures. It is a complete revised screenplay, not a recovered source or a true-story allegation. No talent, producer, financing or distribution is attached.

The screenplay is **91 physical PDF pages: one title page and 90 dramatic pages**, with 54 causal scenes and approximately 14,500 prose words. The development package has 15 content modules across 20 physical pages. All 16 distinct stakeholder pitches are one page each. Page count and artifact checks do not establish artistic quality or performed runtime.

Read [screenplay](../../site/examples/the-ledger-room/screenplay.pdf), [development package](../../site/examples/the-ledger-room/studio-package.pdf), or [general pitch](../../site/examples/the-ledger-room/one-pager.pdf). The remaining public PDFs are in site/examples/the-ledger-room/ at the repository root, and editable here in one_pagers/. A local build creates output/. Editable screenplay sources are screenplay/act1.tex through act6.tex. Shared local fonts and print settings are in fonts/ and common/.

The bounded process was one authoring pass, one full self-diagnosis, one focused repair and real build/layout fixes. A separate native reader read the frozen first draft and a specified revised subset; the parent checked the climax clock/seal chain. Those scopes and unchanged-source hashes are preserved in development/. Neither the reader nor mechanical checks supplied a blanket professional quality certificate. The fictional first-draft text is retained for revision evidence; runtime authoring scripts are omitted from the curated example.

Build from this folder with Python 3, make, either Tectonic or latexmk/XeLaTeX, and Poppler's pdfinfo:

```sh
python3 scripts/setup_check.py --project .
python3 scripts/story_check.py build --project .
python3 scripts/workflow.py status
```

The build uses bundled fonts and relative paths. Building changes PDF hashes and requires refreshed actual visual receipts before delivery. See development/verification.md for all-page contact-sheet inspection and full-resolution samples. The helper's receipts check currency and coverage declarations, not whether a reader actually looked or whether the film works.
