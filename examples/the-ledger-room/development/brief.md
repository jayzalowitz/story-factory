# The Ledger Room
Original contained feature: institutional drama with dry workplace humor. In fictional coastal Bellwether, archivist Clara Venn discovers that a demolished housing estate's compensation schedule was altered while clearing her depot. Temporary courier Jory Senn needs Clara's completion certificate to retain her own depot job. Forty-eight hours. Adult audience; practical moral pressure rather than conspiracy spectacle.
