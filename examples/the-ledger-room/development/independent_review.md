Read-only assessment of the frozen first feature draft, 20 October 2026. I read every actual scene in `feature-demo/development/original-draft/act1.tex` through `act6.tex`, in order: S01–S54, nine scenes per file. No source changes, other reviews, toolkit-wide reading, model CLI calls, or subagents. The supplied 71 PDF pages / 70 dramatic pages are context, not a PDF measurement independently reproduced here. I checked source SHA-256 before and after the reading; all six were unchanged (manifest below).

## Judgment

The story has a complete, coherent chamber-feature spine: finding the book exposes Clara's old false assurance; her attempt to preserve it endangers Jory's employment; Jory's unauthorized disclosure damages the people the intervention purports to help; the eventual success is a properly bounded transfer rather than recovered money. The ending appropriately refuses compensation, employment, or forgiveness as automatic rewards.

The most valuable next pass is substantive relationship and decision development. The present draft's brevity comes partly from withholding encounters the plot has earned, rather than from a missing extra plot. A roughly 90–100-page version could be justified if the four dramatic expansions below earn their space. There is no present story argument for forcing 110 pages. Conversely, 71 pages could serve a deliberately short feature, but it would retain the emotional and causal limits identified here. This is not a universal quality pass or a guarantee of running time.

## What already works

- S04/S09/S15/S25 turn an apparently technical discrepancy into Clara's personal implication. S21–S24 give Jory an actual harmful act, rather than making her a flawless working-class corrective to Clara.
- The women repeatedly have aims beyond assisting the protagonist. Mara leaves; Nell negotiates her own college journey; Bea finishes paid sewing and controls her papers; Sana has nine workers to answer to. S12, S16, S28, S31, S38 and S39 make their limits visible through behavior.
- Lida's S19 floodwall explanation is a strong competing public good. S41 correctly distinguishes authorized account reconciliation from proof that households were paid. Keeping these distinctions prevents an easy villain and an unearned exposé victory.
- The saucer, bad calculator, torn jacket, kettle, receipts, gates and keys carry theme through filmable action. S29–S30 let Tom leave when he said he would. S37 makes Clara acknowledge Jory's cost without dressing it up. S54's different accounts at one steadied table is an earned final image.

## Six prioritized findings for one focused revision

### IFR-01 — High: make Clara's old choice psychologically particular

**Evidence:** S15 exposes her contents-verified tick. S20 supplies the year-end grant deadline and framed commendation. S25 produces her request for missing attachments and her subsequent closure. Yet S26 says she cannot explain why; S32 repeats the confession; S54 still leaves WHY REQUEST CLOSED blank and ends as she begins writing. We know the administrative act and its consequences, but little about what Clara specifically wanted or feared when she did it. The framing of the commendation is suggestive, not an enacted pressure. The exact incriminating leaf also emerges from a pocket of the already-examined blue book at the moment it is needed.

**Repair:** Give the present investigation a sustained encounter in which Clara tries a particular self-serving explanation, meets contradictory evidence or another person's memory, and relinquishes it. Tom is well placed because he remembers the receiving operation and refuses invented certainty. Seed the folded pocket or a cross-reference before S25 so her own search earns the discovery. By the final statement, let the audience hear or see one difficult, specific admission about the reward she accepted or the question she chose not to pursue. She need not recover a perfect memory or identify the paste's author.

**Consequence protected:** The film remains about accountable uncertainty, while Clara becomes a person with a consequential choice rather than a signature carrying the theme. This is the strongest justification for several additional dramatic pages.

### IFR-02 — High: distinguish Jory's available choice from a condition she cannot control

**Evidence:** Clara is the completion signatory in S02; Jory cannot alter that municipal instruction in S21. S35 offers permanent hours conditional on clean sign-off; S36 has Jory refuse to certify clean; S39 withdraws the offer. But Clara has already amended her certificate in S18, so it is unclear what Jory could truthfully do at midnight to secure the job. Her employment loss is credible coercion, but that particular send-button beat risks presenting an unavailable alternative as a freely rejected one. Her strongest real choices are the damaging S21 disclosure and the later decision to document its spread in S23/S34.

**Repair:** Before S36, clarify a concrete option Jory actually controls and its likely employment consequence. Either establish a truthful, workable exit that would leave the disputed transfer unsupported, or place the decisive risk in her insistence that the disclosure incident and contractor conduct enter the record. Let her ask operations the direct question and get an answer. Do not have her falsely certify someone else's contents simply to manufacture a sacrifice. Bring Nell into the practical deliberation rather than having her mostly absorb its result: she can propose an imperfect alternative, disagree, or choose which burden is hers.

**Consequence protected:** Preserve the unfair employer and Jory's actual harm to households, while making her later agency as concrete as her need for employment. Nell gains an independent decision rather than becoming proof that Jory has something to lose.

### IFR-03 — High: put one dissenting affected person on screen

**Evidence:** Bea is unusually well drawn in S12/S17/S22/S38. The other households, however, arrive as her reports: a dead resident's daughter, someone afraid of repayment, an unanswered call, a woman wanting the book destroyed. S54's mother and daughter want different things, but neither is present. Thus the crucial objection to the protagonists' preservation project remains an intelligently stated abstraction mediated by Bea.

**Repair:** Add one bounded encounter with an affected adult who has an immediate life to get back to and refuses the proposed process for a specific reason. Bea should have to negotiate her own urge to organize, not simply transmit a ready-made consent list. The person may decline a submission or permit only a narrow use; let that decision change the contact bundle or the protagonists' plan. A mother/daughter disagreement already seeded by S54 could supply this encounter without multiplying subplots. Avoid a montage of six cases or a new claimant whose purpose is to praise Clara.

**Consequence protected:** The distinction between preserving a record and conscripting a person becomes dramatic action. Bea acquires a relationship and a fallible organizing decision beyond opposing or educating Clara. This expansion also gives household privacy a human cost beyond the circulated image.

### IFR-04 — Medium-high: earn the Clara–Jory partnership after the disclosure breach

**Evidence:** The early handling rhythm, S09 employment rupture, S18 argument and S23 mutual accusation are strong. Both apologize to Bea in S26. In S27, though, they promptly become a joint problem-solving unit again; the remaining friction is chiefly their shared procedural work. S33's dinner and S37's acknowledgment are good but very brief. The central relationship has room for a negotiated repair, not merely parallel accountability.

**Repair:** Expand the S26–S28 hinge into a contested plan. Jory should require Clara to stop committing other people's time and income before asking them; Clara should require a concrete disclosure safeguard rather than accepting good intentions. Let each cede one practical authority to the other, then test those terms during inventory or transport. Use a later moment to show one of them observing the agreement when urgency makes breaking it attractive. Keep their connection grounded in work; a friendship speech or instant forgiveness would weaken it.

**Consequence protected:** Their final coordination becomes the result of a changed relationship. The same material can carry the action, conflict and intimacy needed for a longer feature, rather than adding unrelated scenes. Develop IFR-02 and IFR-04 together so they do not become two versions of the same argument.

### IFR-05 — Medium: establish the custody route early and honor the climax's stated clock

**Evidence:** S10 says Mina can receive objects but not a department's unfinished business, and directs Clara toward an outside secure room. S28 newly reveals the municipal strongroom route for ballot returns. S33 then offers the strongroom once the owner authorizes release and an interim custodian. The genuine difference between this route and ordinary damaged storage is inferable but arrives in pieces, making the middle risk feel partly generated by undisclosed options. More concretely, S33 holds the slot until 08:45; S46 is 08:42, S47 enters the strongroom at 08:47 and records receipt at 08:53 without anyone acknowledging the expired hold.

**Repair:** At S10 establish what prevents registered strongroom custody now: a specific missing owner authorization, eligible custodian, or itemized linked series. S28 can then reveal how to satisfy a known obstacle rather than introduce the escape route. Clarify why ordinary warehouse preservation would leave the operative dispute unregistered. At the climax, either define 08:45 as the counter-presentation deadline (met at 08:31), explicitly allow Mina to continue processing an admitted consignment, or revise the clock. Keep the seal issue, but ensure the incorporated condition sheet accounts for Lida's existing signature as well as the people signing at the counter.

**Consequence protected:** Preserve credible custody and restricted access without turning the screenplay into regulatory exposition. The audience can anticipate the obstacle, understand how the characters solve it, and trust the deadlines.

### IFR-06 — Medium: give the dry voices different engines and let the denouement spend less time qualifying status

**Evidence:** Nearly everyone produces compact antitheses or corrective definitions: Clara's noun distinctions, Jory's format jokes, Bea's distinctions between numbers and people, Mina's form taxonomy, Sana's limits on promises, Lida's precise limits of authority. Individual examples are often excellent; their concentration makes disparate people sound like fluent co-authors of the same argument. S48–S53 then repeatedly distinguishes received from verified, opened from won, an authorized transfer from paid households, and a meeting without a date from rejection. All are truthful, but several scenes resolve to a similar explanatory beat.

**Repair:** Keep Clara's exact labels, Mina's form-based brevity and Jory's material improvisation. Let Bea sometimes pursue a concrete demand without producing the best aphorism; let Lida evade or use longer administrative speech when cornered; let Nell speak from what she is doing. In the final sequence, make each scene deliver a distinct changed action: lost room, independent departure, consent decision, request for living costs, actual commute, written responsibility. Trim dialogue that restates a limit already embodied in those actions. Retain S54's image and divergent accounts.

**Consequence protected:** The humor stays dry and the unresolved outcomes stay honest, while the characters sound less interchangeable and the ending gathers force rather than repeatedly certifying its caution. This is a sharpening pass, not an expansion target.

## Expansion boundary and remaining limits

The first four findings suggest several sustained confrontations rather than extra errands: Clara/Tom over her old closure; Jory/operations and Nell over what choice remains; Bea with a dissenting household; Clara/Jory setting terms for renewed cooperation. Depending on execution these could earn approximately 18–28 dramatic pages while some repetitive qualification is cut. That supports exploring roughly 90–100 pages; it is not a quota or evidence that page count alone improves the film. Keep the existing limited production footprint and reuse the depot, flats, layby and service office where possible. One additional affected adult is more valuable than a crowd or a public-hearing finale.

The decisive success should remain preserved, properly described records and a truthful officer statement. Do not solve the compensation review, restore Jory's contract as a reward, turn Lida into the paste's culprit, or have Bea grant general forgiveness. My reading assesses causality and dramatic development from the complete source. It does not establish legal accuracy in a named jurisdiction, production budget, performed runtime, or audience response. No second reading or review of a revised draft was performed.
