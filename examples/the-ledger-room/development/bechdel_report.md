# Named-women Bechdel assessment
Verdict: PASS
Scope: full current54-scene draft, identity confirmed by the explicit development roster rather than names. The screenplay and roster identify Clara, Jory, Bea, Lida, Ina, Mara, Mina, Sana and Nell as women/girl.

S22: Ina addresses Bea about her rent slips, consent and workplace intrusion. Ina: "You don't get to put it in a file because it's true." Bea: "You asked me to keep those rent slips." Ina: "To keep them." Bea returns the papers and removes the story. They address each other; the subject is records/consent, not a particular man. Clara participates but is not required for the qualifying exchange.

S27 independently qualifies: Jory asks Clara whether her overtime gets paid, asks for the signed time sheet and imposes disclosure/exit terms. Clara supplies the sheet and agrees. These are women's decisions about labor and evidence, not merely two names in a cast list.

Broader agency is separately assessed: Ina's refusal removes useful testimony; Bea loses representative control; Jory owns a late economic choice; Mara and Nell preserve their own futures; Lida acts for an independent public obligation. The binary pass does not prove those characters are well written, feminist representation, general quality or professional readiness. No token repair was added for this test: the consent conflict is necessary to the central custody plan.
