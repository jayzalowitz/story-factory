# Premise review
Verdict: PASS
Hold scope. The ambitious version would stage housing demolition and a public inquiry; the contained version shows how those offscreen acts survive in receipts, habits and rooms. A physically closing building makes inaction an action. Main risks: procedural exposition, a too-convenient rescue rule, and a virtuous archivist. Countermeasures: disputed bundles require a named custodian and personal exposure; Clara helped create the defective record; Jory may refuse the ethical plan. The ending preserves evidence but promises no compensation or job restoration.
