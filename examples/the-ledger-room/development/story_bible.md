# Story bible
The title names the working room that outlasts its building: who can enter, write and correct a record. Logline: During a forty-eight-hour records-depot closure, an archivist who finds an altered housing compensation list must persuade a courier dependent on her clean clearance to help preserve evidence that implicates her own career.

Dramatic question: Can Clara put the dispute into lawful custody before the physical record and the workers' livelihoods are converted into a completed job? Her defense is immaculate procedure with a narrowed field of responsibility. Her change is signing a factual account of her own omission and relinquishing control over others' claims. Jory offers practical movement and bargaining; she refuses to become unpaid conscience. Ina Werr refuses contact and retrieves private papers; her removal of a useful account challenges Bea's representative power. Bea wants evidence returned to residents under conditions they choose, including residents with awkward claims. Lida thinks a solvent town is a good worth dirty hands; she does not control the independent custody registry. Mina can receive a disputed transfer but cannot decide compensation or save employment.

Tensions: Does a true record require exposure? Is a job bought with silence still something a worker may defend? Who pays for a public body's correction? Can proof preserve the right to be unrepresented?

Ending: Clara admits the blank schedule she certified, has her bridge payment held, and becomes a temporary personal custodian of the sealed bundle; Jory completes a disputed rather than clean transfer and loses the promised permanent post. Bea retains her receipt and the consent conditions; Mina retains both strongroom keys. A review opens without a restitution promise. Clara works beside a former resident instead of owning the interpretation.

Linear, present day, close to Clara and Jory; information is obtained through action, not omniscient flashbacks. Six locations, two vehicles, no demolition or storm spectacle. Humor comes from workplace competence and resistance. The machine that stamps copies, the numbered seals and the crooked shelf recur only when their function changes.
