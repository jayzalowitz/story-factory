\textbf{Status: creative search brief, no named shortlist or attachments.} This module adapts the target list to the project's current stage rather than inventing approaches or availability.

\subsection{Aspirational profile}
An established director who can sustain moral ambiguity in ordinary work, with an ensemble rather than a single-star hierarchy. The central test is whether S22's dissent remains uncomfortable after the protagonists offer practical help. Evaluate staging and listening, not just reputation for social themes.

\subsection{Practical independent profile}
A director with strong actor rehearsal practice and evidence of making limited locations change dramatic function. Ask for a scene-based approach to S27 and S48: one sets a conditional partnership; the other makes a small interface choice consequential without turning a screen into spectacle.

\subsection{Emerging profile}
An accomplished short-form or theater director with a demonstrated grasp of screen geography and work behavior, supported by a producer experienced in feature scheduling. A low location count does not remove the need for coverage planning and editorial rhythm.
