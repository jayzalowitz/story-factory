\section{Producer Search Profiles}
No company or individual has been approached, attached or represented as interested. Current roles and mandates have not been researched. This is an assembly brief.

\textbf{Lead creative producer.} Seek experience developing actor-led contained features, with willingness to preserve the absence of a clean restitution ending. The producer should obtain actual reader feedback, resolve the runtime/length question and make one coherent revision strategy before packaging names.

\textbf{Physical production partner.} Seek access to controllable civic or industrial interiors and experience making a working depot safe and usable. The principal savings assumption is reuse of room clusters, not free municipal access. Nine loaded cartons, vehicles, night work and paper continuity require planning.

\textbf{Financing partner.} Seek a partner who can assess a modest adult drama without relying on invented returns or awards prospects. No financing model is supplied until a line budget, schedule and casting approach exist.

\textbf{Community/access partner.} An archive or labor-oriented organization could advise on work behavior and host an optional discussion, with paid time and no obligation to endorse. Consultation must not turn real residents into publicity proof of this fictional account.

Next steps are a professional script read, budget/schedule, location feasibility, rights and chain-of-title review for the actual production, then a verified shortlist. This package is not evidence that those steps have occurred.
