\section{Awards Considerations}
There is no awards campaign, eligibility determination or prediction. A draft screenplay cannot substantiate a nomination prospect. Current rules, dates and release requirements would need verification for any chosen program.

If a completed film earns distribution and resources, the demonstrable craft discussion could concern ensemble listening, restrained performance, production design that makes work legible, and sound built from civic labor. Clara's admission, Ina's refusal and Jory's final status decision would be specific scenes to discuss rather than generalized claims of importance.

The practical priority is the film: strong performances, clarity of the custody problem and an ending that does not pretend a preserved record has repaired a life. Do not change character age, simplify consent or add a public speech to create a supposed showcase. Any later campaign should be proportionate to actual reception and available funds, with truthful credits and accessibility materials.
