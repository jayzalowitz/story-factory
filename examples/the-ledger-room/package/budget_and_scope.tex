\section{Production Scope and Budget Assumptions}
\textbf{No line budget or monetary range has been prepared.} The project is designed as a contained independent feature. Contained does not mean cheap, financed or feasible at a particular number.

Six practical sites: records depot; courier depot; Bea's flat; municipal service office; Clara's flat; bridge layby. Several rooms are redresses or zones within the two civic/industrial sites. Approximately ten meaningful speaking roles, plus a phone operations voice and minimal background. Nell's calls can be neutral inserts. The bus boarding can stay at the layby; no moving-bus interior is necessary.

Main cost drivers: controlled night access, vehicle and pedestrian safety, shelf/ladder work, production documents and privacy-clear inserts, consistent seals and cartons, room lighting over changing hours, cast availability and the sustained ensemble encounter at Bea's. No demolition, disaster effect, courtroom crowd or period reconstruction appears.

A practical plan should test the depot gate turn, carton weights, van capacity, access to the reading-counter and strongroom sets, and copying/phone-screen workflows before scheduling. Use safe loads and qualified handling plans. A real producer and department heads must determine duration, insurance, permits, pay and contingency. No incentives or public funding qualification is assumed.
