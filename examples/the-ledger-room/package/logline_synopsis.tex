\subsection{Short synopsis}
Bellwether's records depot closes in forty-eight hours. Clara Venn needs its clean transfer to release her retirement bridge payment. Jory Senn, the temporary courier, needs the same certificate for permanent hours and a room near her daughter's college. An altered housing ledger breaks their common plan. Investigating six held balances exposes Clara's own unchecked certification and Jory's damaging attempt to force an answer through her employer. They secure disputed custody on time, with resident consent kept separate. The record survives; Jory's permanent offer lapses and Clara's immediate payment is held. Compensation remains unproved and unpaid.

\subsection{Full synopsis}
Clara's last week is organized into cartons, seals and signatures. Jory arrives with a van and an employment offer contingent on satisfactory closure. Their competent, abrasive cooperation uncovers a pasted settlement column in the Low Steps relocation ledger. A surviving carbon marks six households held rather than settled. Bea Quill, a former resident seeking the return of her stair rods, has a receipt for a bad moving van but no proof of compensation.

Clara declares the records disputed. The completion system immediately threatens Jory's job. Municipal transition director Lida Rusk offers ordinary damaged-record storage and argues that residents may have elected a moving allowance. The supposed election packet is empty, yet Clara signed that its contents were verified. Her own missing-attachments request shows she later closed the question to meet a deadline.

Jory sends a photograph of the ledger to operations to gain leverage. It circulates, frightening residents and causing access suspension. Ina Werr retrieves private rent slips and makes Bea strike her story from the packet. Bea cannot speak for all six households. Clara and Jory must report their own failures rather than borrow her authority. The linked evidence requires nine cartons with different privacy restrictions.

Courier storage cannot guarantee continuity. A municipal strongroom can receive a properly declared transfer only with owner authorization and a present officer's factual statement. Lida signs narrow access after Clara accepts exposure of her old certification and a hold on her bridge payment. Lida's separately authorized flood-reserve transfer enters the packet too: account settlement does not establish household payment, but neither does a pasted word prove a theft.

On Thursday morning the women finish the inventory, record resident refusal as well as consent, and replace a faulty seal. Mina Olt registers the nine cartons at08:53. Clara's public officer statement retains her own name while household material stays restricted. Jory registers completion with exception. The building is cleared before nine; the employment condition is not fulfilled.

Afterward Mara leaves for her own kitchen job, Nell commutes, Jory takes casual shifts and opens a grievance, and Clara seeks housing advice while her payment is held. The reserve audit passes; the household review remains open. At a smaller reading table Clara and Bea write separate accounts. They share a stable surface, not a single account of what happened.
