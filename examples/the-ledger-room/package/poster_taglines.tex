\section{Tagline Options}
\tagline{Everything accounted for. Except the people.}
\tagline{The job is finished. The record is open.}
\tagline{Some things won't fit under one signature.}
\tagline{Two women. Nine boxes. No clean ending.}
\tagline{A correction has to go somewhere.}
These are alternative creative directions, not tested audience claims. The first emphasizes the title's moral question; the second the procedural irony; the third the central relationship; the fourth the contained ensemble; the fifth the practical pursuit. Avoid implying an identified criminal or a compensation victory.
