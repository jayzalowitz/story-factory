Actor Pitch
\end{center}
\textbf{The role} Clara Venn,54, an archivist whose control is useful until it becomes an excuse. She wants an orderly retirement. An old receiving certificate makes her the best witness and a compromised witness at once. The permanent key she once treasured is tied to the question she chose to close.

\textbf{Playable turns} S10: push back the option that would expose her. S25: find the closed query, remember pleasure without manufacturing chronology. S34: acknowledge the financial hold while a lawful safer alternative remains. S54: write the particular self-protective reason instead of performing generalized remorse.

\textbf{The ensemble} Jory bargains in labor and disclosure terms; Bea is not available as a grateful victim; Mara leaves despite affection. Clara cannot win by being the cleverest speaker. Listening, embarrassment, domestic impatience and physical work matter as much as confession.

\textbf{Proposal} Read Clara with an ensemble partner and test the counter and final-table scenes. Age and behavior should fit the long career and adult daughter. No performer is attached, approached or assumed available. This is a creative role proposal, not a casting offer.
