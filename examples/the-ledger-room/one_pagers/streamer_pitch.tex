Streamer Pitch

\textbf{Attention engine} A forty-eight-hour task supplies a clear line: finish a depot transfer before the building changes hands. Each answer changes the task, from one altered book to nine privacy-classed cartons and the workers' own incident records. The central relationship remains present while the institutional problem grows.

\textbf{Viewer experience} The film offers practical competence, moral discomfort and dry relief. Its ending resolves custody and refuses to pretend that a receipt creates compensation or employment. Small scenes with Mara and Nell keep the costs personal without making daughters rewards for the protagonists.

\textbf{Platform fit remains hypothetical} Potential relevance to viewers of ensemble workplace and social dramas is a creative hypothesis. No platform demand, viewing data, current commissioning mandate, runtime classification or algorithmic advantage has been verified.

Any acquisition discussion would require an actual team, budget, rights package and finished-film or production evidence.
