\textbf{Appropriate scope} There is no campaign or awards prediction. This is a draft craft brief for a possible future completed film. Eligibility and release rules would need verification for any named program.

\textbf{Demonstrable opportunities} Clara's permanent-key memory is specific without proving a convenient chronology. Jory's final normal-return option is genuinely available. Ina's sustained refusal denies the protagonists a useful witness. These are performance and ensemble opportunities, not evidence of a future nomination.

\textbf{Craft proposition} Production design must make an archive functional; sound follows gates, tape, wheels and keys; editing must preserve legibility without turning every document into an insert. The final table steadied by two people lets the film close on action and divergent accounts.

\textbf{Next step} Make the film work. Only after actual reception and resources exist should a producer consider a proportionate campaign with truthful credits and claims. No artistic prize, critical response, eligibility or professional endorsement is promised in this package.
