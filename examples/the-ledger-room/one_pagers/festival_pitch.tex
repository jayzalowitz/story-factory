Festival Pitch
\end{center}
\textbf{Artistic identity} A contained contemporary drama about the cost of making a correction accessible. Its form follows work rather than a public revelation: labeling, lifting, reading and refusing a signature. The preserved record remains capable of supporting contradictory accounts.

\textbf{Programming conversation} The film could contribute to programs interested in labor, institutions, memory or intimate ensemble performance. S22 gives the affected person who declines participation an actual scene and a consequence; S48 makes a small carrier-status decision carry the employment cost.

\textbf{Boundaries} Bellwether and all its people and procedures are fictional. No selection, premiere status, eligibility, current deadline or endorsement is claimed. The project is a revised screenplay demonstration, not a completed film ready to submit.

\textbf{Next step} An artistic script read now; later a verified festival strategy based on the finished film, resources and release obligations. If discussions accompany a screening, invite archive and labor voices on their own terms. The film does not offer a simple lesson or claim to represent real residents.
