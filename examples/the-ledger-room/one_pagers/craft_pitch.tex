Craft Department Pitch
\end{center}
\textbf{Design problem} Make the paper trail usable and differentiated: ledger, carbon, receiving cover, query register, consent envelope, seal condition sheet and carrier interface each serve a distinct action. A beautiful wall of documents would defeat the story if viewers cannot follow who is deciding.

\textbf{Scene anchors} S25: the scale test must look suggestive and explicitly inconclusive before the query register supplies the real evidence. S38: a separately carried envelope changes consent handling. S43: the replacement seal needs clear repeatable continuity. S48: normal and exception statuses must be immediately legible, without invented digital magic.

\textbf{Sound and costume} A square trolley wheel, copy feeder, gate and two-key strongroom can carry pressure. Jory's jacket tears, is stapled, then sewn; the repair survives into ordinary commuting. The saucer changes use without changing appearance.

\textbf{Next step} Create a practical document and prop continuity test with safe dummy loads, then plan inserts and screen text with the director and editor. No department head or supplier is attached. Jurisdiction, safety and production specifications await a real plan.
