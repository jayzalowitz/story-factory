General Pitch
\end{center}
\textbf{The film} An archivist and a temporary courier have forty-eight hours to close the same depot. A clean completion releases Clara's retirement bridge payment and Jory's permanent employment. An altered housing ledger makes that signature untrue. The pursuit becomes nine linked cartons, a lawful receiving place and a record of their own compromised acts.

\textbf{The experience} A contained institutional drama with dry workplace wit: pleasure in competence becomes discomfort about what competence protects. The former resident who refuses to participate changes the packet. The director who authorized a flood-reserve transfer remains a person defending a public good, not an exposed mastermind.

\textbf{The ending and scale} Evidence reaches custody on time. Compensation is not adjudicated, the permanent job lapses and the bridge payment is held. Six sites, a focused ensemble, contemporary setting and no demolition spectacle. Complete revised AI-authored demonstration draft; actual pages and inspection limits are in the handoff.

\textbf{Proposed next step} A human script read and a scene-specific conversation about pacing, followed by budget and location work. No attachments or financing are represented.
