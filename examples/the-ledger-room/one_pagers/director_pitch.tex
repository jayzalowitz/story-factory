\textbf{Formal opportunity} Make paper and interfaces legible through decisions rather than montage. Two workers must complete a practical job while the definition of completion changes. Six locations recur with altered authority: the same counter can be a service barrier, a confession space and a receiving point.

\textbf{Anchor sequences} S22 is a sustained confrontation in a working flat: Ina refuses a useful story and Bea must let go of the papers. S27 rebuilds a partnership through paid-time and privacy terms. S48 presents a button that could preserve the book and secure a job while closing an unresolved incident. No triumphant cue can do that acting.

\textbf{Approach} A near-linear forty-eight-hour clock, ordinary coastal light and sound made from labor. Keep supporting people capable of ending an encounter. The final table supports different accounts; it does not reconcile them into one lesson.

\textbf{Next step} Discuss an actor-rehearsal and coverage plan, particularly the long flat scene and small terminal choice. Complete revised demonstration draft; no director attached. Production feasibility and audience response remain to be tested.
