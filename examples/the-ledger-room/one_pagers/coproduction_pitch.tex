Co-production Pitch
\end{center}
\textbf{Why this might travel} The invented coastal municipality avoids an allegation about a real authority. Records, conditional work and contested household representation can be legible across borders. That portability is a creative premise, not evidence of treaty qualification or international sales.

\textbf{Contributions to explore} An actual partner might supply controllable depot and civic interiors, ensemble casting, department expertise or postproduction. The story should not acquire additional locations or a public hearing merely to satisfy hypothetical contribution requirements.

\textbf{Open issues} Language, jurisdiction, labor terms, rights, financing split, creative control and any national or treaty tests are unresolved. No incentive, fund eligibility, qualifying spend or partner availability has been checked. Fictional procedure still needs coherent professional consultation in the chosen production world.

\textbf{Proposed next step} A feasibility conversation after a lead producer and real budget exist. Map genuine creative and physical contributions before evaluating co-production structures. No partner has been contacted and this draft does not constitute a financial or legal structure.
