Cultural Partnership Pitch
\end{center}
\textbf{Possible collaboration} A completed fictional film about archives, work and consent could support a discussion with an archive, library, labor group or community arts organization. The useful question is not whether records matter, but who can make a correction and who pays for making it.

\textbf{Mutual benefit} A partner could shape a paid consultation or later moderated event using its own expertise. The production could provide accessible screening materials and a clear account of what the film invents. Neither side should be required to endorse the other's claims.

\textbf{Boundaries in the story and partnership} Ina refuses to become evidence for an organizer's project; that boundary should govern actual engagement too. Do not recruit real residents to authenticate an invented allegation. Do not extract personal stories for publicity without specific consent and terms.

\textbf{Next step} An exploratory conversation only after a real producer and resources exist. No organization is attached, approached or represented as interested. Events, compensation, accessibility and permissions would be concrete agreements, not assumptions created by this pitch.
