## Unit 1: First Flight Landing
https://www.nps.gov/places/000/first-flight-landing.htm
Historical paragraph, page updated May 2, 2021.
On December 17, 1903, around 10:35 a.m., Orville piloted a flight lasting twelve seconds and traveling 120 feet. The location is Kill Devil Hills, North Carolina. These modest measurements are the basis of the short's first visible flight; the page does not document private conversation or the brothers' motives.

## Unit 2: Fourth Flight Landing
https://www.nps.gov/places/000/fourth-flight-landing.htm
Main historical paragraph and “Why No Fifth Flight?”, updated May 2, 2021.
Wilbur made the fourth flight that day, covering 852 feet in 59 seconds. Afterwards wind overturned the machine and damaged its engine, ending the experiments for that season. The account describes plans to visit the postmaster after lunch. It does not establish the wording, timing or location of any private discussion.

## Unit 3: The First Flight
https://www.nps.gov/articles/firstflight.htm
Historical narrative, paragraphs beginning “On December 17” through “An enormous advancement”; updated August 15, 2017.
A repaired machine, strong wind, a signal to lifesaving-station volunteers and a handshake preceded Orville's attempt. John Daniels operated a preset camera. Four flights followed in all. Wind later damaged the flyer; the brothers sent their father a telegram about the results. The article supports a concise event sequence but is not a transcript. Its timing differs in precision rather than substance from Unit 1: 10:35 versus approximately 10:35. This short avoids claiming second-level precision.
