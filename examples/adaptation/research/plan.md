# Bounded historical research plan

Question: which public event facts can support a compact short about the Wright brothers' December 17, 1903 flights without pretending to know their private conversation?

Scope: the day's first and fourth flights, alternation, damage and later message. Exclude a full invention-of-flight priority investigation, family biography, exact original telegram wording and private motives. Budget: up to four official source candidates; one sequential pass by the authoring agent. No helper teams, outside model services or paid access.

Assignments actually executed by one agent: chronology (flight order and measurements); relationships (documented participants versus invented private exchanges); context (wind, launching method, damage and message). Native workers did not run. Decisive evidence must be opened and read before receiving verified status. Research limitations remain in the final audit.
