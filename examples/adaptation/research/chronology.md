# Chronology

December 17, 1903: first flight by Orville, approximately 10:35 a.m., 120 feet/12 seconds (C001). The men alternate for four total flights (S3); fourth by Wilbur, 852 feet/59 seconds (C002). Wind damages the flyer after the last flight, ending the season, and the brothers later send their father a telegram (C003). Exact timing/location of invented private scenes is not documented and is not certified here.
