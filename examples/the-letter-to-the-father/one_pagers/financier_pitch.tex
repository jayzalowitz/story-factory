\textbf{Audience and Positioning}\\
An adult literary drama with procedural traction from Kafka's insurance work, a father-letter spine, and a warmer Berlin/Dora movement. Illustrative comparables include \textit{The Brutalist}, \textit{Tár}, \textit{Maestro} and \textit{Phantom Thread}. Similarity is a positioning hypothesis, not proof of audience demand or revenue.

\textbf{Release Hypothesis}\\
A festival-led introduction, limited theatrical release and later streaming sale could suit the work. No festival invitation, distributor interest, sales estimate or awards outcome is established. A campaign would depend on the completed film, eligibility, response and available resources.

\textbf{Production Assumptions}\\
The historical development plan proposed a Prague-centered shoot and European co-production. Period detail, locations, performer availability and the six-movement structure need a line producer's breakdown. Incentive eligibility and rates must be researched again for the actual production; no rebate is secured by this package.
