\section{Producer and Studio Targets}

The best homes are companies that understand three things at once: actor-forward prestige, European co-production reality, and how to market this as more than literary reverence --- the office machine, the father-letter title object, and the late Berlin warmth are the usable hooks.

\target{Plan B Entertainment (Brad Pitt)}{Track record with prestige films about human beings under systemic pressure: \textit{12 Years a Slave}, \textit{Moonlight}, \textit{The Big Short}. Consistent pipeline to awards recognition. Strong relationships with prestige distributors and a real appetite for psychologically exact material.}{Dream / Strong Fit}

\target{A24}{The definitive prestige indie label of the current era. \textit{The Zone of Interest}, \textit{The Whale}, \textit{Everything Everywhere}. Their brand is aligned not just with formal ambition but with severe, actor-forward films that can be sold through a clean title hook and a distinct atmosphere.}{Strong Fit}

\target{Searchlight Pictures}{Disney's prestige label. Strong track record with actor-forward dramas and literary-adjacent prestige work: \textit{The Shape of Water}, \textit{Nomadland}, \textit{The Banshees of Inisherin}. They have the distribution infrastructure to turn a controlled, severe film into a full awards campaign.}{Strong Fit}

\target{Focus Features}{Universal's specialty label. Prestige drama is their core business: \textit{T\'{a}r}, \textit{Phantom Thread}, \textit{Atonement}. Experienced with European co-productions, period logistics, and films whose commercial value lives in adult seriousness plus major performance bait.}{Realistic}

\target{Netflix Film}{Aggressive awards strategy. Following investments in \textit{The Power of the Dog}, \textit{Maestro}, \textit{All Quiet on the Western Front}. Platform release model could be advantageous for a film of this scale. Strong international production infrastructure.}{Realistic}

\target{MUBI}{Emerging as a serious player in prestige distribution. Their acquisition of \textit{Aftersun} and production pipeline suggest appetite for formally ambitious, critically acclaimed work with strong auteur positioning. Especially plausible if the package closes with a more director-driven lane.}{Stretch}

\target{BBC Film}{Long history of prestige literary adaptation and European co-production. Key partner for UK-European financing structures. Institutional credibility in the prestige space.}{Realistic}

\target{Film4}{Channel 4's film arm. Track record includes \textit{12 Years a Slave}, \textit{Room}, \textit{The Zone of Interest}. Consistently backs formally ambitious, awards-competitive projects.}{Strong Fit}

\target{The Match Factory}{European sales and production. Represents many of the directors on our target list. Deep expertise in Central European prestige production, difficult adult material, and the exact co-production structures this film would likely use.}{Realistic / Strong Fit}

\target{Protagonist Pictures}{European sales and financing. Strong festival relationships. Experienced with the specific production landscape this film requires.}{Realistic}

\target{Andrew Macdonald / DNA Films}{Produced \textit{28 Days Later}, \textit{Ex Machina}, \textit{Never Let Me Go}. Experienced with prestige genre-adjacent material. Practical, awards-literate, European production expertise.}{Realistic}

\target{See-Saw Films (Emile Sherman / Iain Canning)}{Produced \textit{The King's Speech}, \textit{Lion}, \textit{The Power of the Dog}. Exceptional track record with prestige drama, international co-production, and films that pair formal control with emotional accessibility.}{Strong Fit}
