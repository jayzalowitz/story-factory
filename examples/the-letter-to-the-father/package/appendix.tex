\section{Risk Factors and Mitigations}

\risk{Period production costs exceed budget}{Czech Republic production base with 20\% tax rebate, combined with German and Austrian incentives, provides 15--25\% effective cost reduction. Interior-heavy shooting minimizes expensive exterior period reconstruction.}

\risk{``Literary biopic'' market fatigue}{The film's six-movement structure, recurring office case, returned father-letter, and disciplined formal pressure differentiate it decisively from conventional biographical drama. Marketing must emphasize dramatic engines and experiential quality over cradle-to-grave biography.}

\risk{Kafka perceived as inaccessible or academic}{Brody's star power provides the accessible entry point. The film is fundamentally about family, love, illness, and the terror of being known --- universal experiences that happen to be refracted through Kafka's specific life.}

\risk{Competitive Best Actor field}{The Brody narrative (comeback, career culmination, physical transformation) is unusually strong. The role's range --- comedy, terror, romance, physical collapse --- provides objective differentiation. Multiple clip-worthy scenes give the campaign maximum flexibility.}

\risk{Director attachment delays}{The target list includes both dream and realistic options. The script is strong enough to attract talent at multiple tiers. Parallel outreach to directors and producers accelerates the attachment timeline.}

\risk{International financing complexity}{An experienced co-production team (line producer, legal, sales agent) mitigates structural risk. The Czech/German/Austrian corridor is well-established and well-understood.}

\risk{Runtime concerns}{The film targets approximately 110 minutes, which is within the standard range for prestige drama. The six-movement structure provides natural internal pacing and clean internal turns. If festival or distributor feedback suggests tightening, the modular structure allows targeted editing without structural damage.}
