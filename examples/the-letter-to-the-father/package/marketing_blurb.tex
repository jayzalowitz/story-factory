\section{Marketing and Press Positioning}

\subsection{Key Selling Points}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{The Brody Factor}: Adrien Brody delivers the performance of his career as Franz Kafka --- the role he was born to play.
\item \textbf{The Formal Innovation}: Not a conventional biopic but a six-movement prestige drama where office bureaucracy, family life, love, and illness all tighten around the same undeliverable sentence.
\item \textbf{The Dramatic Spine}: The recurring office case, the returned Letter to His Father, and the late Berlin movement give the film more propulsion and emotional shape than a generic literary portrait.
\item \textbf{The Literary Prestige}: Based on one of the most significant writers of the twentieth century, drawing from Kafka's diaries, letters, the famous Letter to His Father, and the final prose that made his life legible.
\item \textbf{The Awards Proposition}: Designed for the highest tier of festival and Academy consideration.
\item \textbf{The Universal Themes}: Family pressure, the terror of intimacy, the body's betrayal, the soul trapped inside systems --- these are not period concerns but permanent human conditions.
\end{itemize}
