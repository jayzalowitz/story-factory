\vfill\small Inspired by the modest scale of early flight experiments, not by an actual musical event. All people, places, dialogue and events are invented. No historical person is represented.\normalsize

FADE IN:\par
\scene{INT. EMPTY SWIMMING POOL - AFTERNOON}

A drained municipal pool. Pale blue walls. JO, sixties, lowers a cello case down the shallow-end steps.\par

INEZ, also sixties, stands at the deep end with a pocket recorder.\par

\character{INEZ}
\begin{quote}Say something.\par\end{quote}

\character{JO}
\begin{quote}I've carried the cello down.\par\end{quote}

The room returns a soft, elongated cello down.\par

\character{INEZ}
\begin{quote}Good.\par\end{quote}

\character{JO}
\begin{quote}For whom?\par\end{quote}

Inez places the recorder on an upturned bucket. Jo opens the case. Inside, a yellow scarf cushions the instrument's neck.\par

\character{INEZ}
\begin{quote}Still got that.\par\end{quote}

\character{JO}
\begin{quote}It's a very reliable scarf.\par\end{quote}

Jo sits on a folding stool. Tunes a string. The room seems to listen.\par

Inez takes a small recorder from her pocket. The musical kind. She tests two notes, quietly. Jo looks up, surprised.\par

\character{INEZ}
\begin{quote}For when I stop singing.\par\end{quote}

\character{JO}
\begin{quote}Planning an emergency exit?\par\end{quote}

Inez puts it on the bucket, beside the other recorder.\par

% scene: S002
\scene{INT. EMPTY SWIMMING POOL - MOMENTS LATER}

Inez presses record. A small red light holds steady.\par

Jo begins a spare original phrase. Inez takes a breath to enter.\par

Nothing comes.\par

Jo finishes the phrase. Waits with her bow raised.\par

\character{INEZ}
\begin{quote}Wrong note.\par\end{quote}

\character{JO}
\begin{quote}Which one?\par\end{quote}

\character{INEZ}
\begin{quote}The one I was going to sing.\par\end{quote}

Jo lowers the bow.\par

\character{INEZ}
\begin{quote}Again.\par\end{quote}

They begin. Inez finds the first note. Her voice breaks on the second.\par

She reaches for the recorder. Jo sets her bow on her knees.\par

\character{JO}
\begin{quote}I stopped playing. That's on there too.\par\end{quote}

Inez takes her hand away from the recorder.\par

\character{INEZ}
\begin{quote}It's your last afternoon here. I'd like something decent.\par\end{quote}

\character{JO}
\begin{quote}I'm moving. I'm not becoming a saint.\par\end{quote}

Inez looks up at the high windows. A little embarrassed by the size of the room she chose.\par

\character{INEZ}
\begin{quote}It sounded better in my head.\par\end{quote}

\character{JO}
\begin{quote}Most rooms do.\par\end{quote}

Jo sets the bow across her knees. She waits without offering another beginning.\par

% scene: S003
\scene{INT. EMPTY SWIMMING POOL - LATER}

Inez plays back the recording. Cello. A breath. A fractured note. Silence.\par

Then Jo's voice, thin through the speaker.\par

\character{JO (RECORDED)}
\begin{quote}I stopped playing. That's on there too.\par\end{quote}

Inez stops the playback. Jo folds the yellow scarf, slowly.\par

\character{INEZ}
\begin{quote}You could stay for the singing on Thursdays.\par\end{quote}

\character{JO}
\begin{quote}And leave again every Friday?\par\end{quote}

Inez nods. She already knows.\par

Jo places the scarf back in the case.\par

\character{INEZ}
\begin{quote}I don't know who starts us without you.\par\end{quote}

\character{JO}
\begin{quote}You do.\par\end{quote}

Inez looks at the little wind instrument. Picks it up. Turns it once between her palms.\par

Then puts it away.\par

She moves the recording bucket closer to the stool. No longer tries to put the whole pool between them.\par

\character{INEZ}
\begin{quote}Don't stop when I do that.\par\end{quote}

\character{JO}
\begin{quote}When you do what?\par\end{quote}

\character{INEZ}
\begin{quote}You know.\par\end{quote}

Jo lifts the bow.\par

% scene: S004
\scene{INT. EMPTY SWIMMING POOL - CONTINUOUS}

Inez presses record.\par

Jo begins the phrase again. Inez enters. The note catches. Jo keeps playing.\par

Inez holds the rough edge instead of fleeing it. Finds the next note. Jo changes her bow's pressure to meet the smaller voice.\par

A short duet. Neither woman looks toward the recorder.\par

Their final sound goes up the pale blue walls and comes back changed.\par

Inez lets the room finish before she presses stop.\par

Jo sets the cello in its case. This time Inez helps, holding the scarf away from the closing lid.\par

\character{INEZ}
\begin{quote}May I play that on Thursday?\par\end{quote}

Jo nods.\par

\character{JO}
\begin{quote}Use both names.\par\end{quote}

Inez presses record again and raises the recorder to her mouth.\par

\character{INEZ}
\begin{quote}Jo and Inez. In the pool.\par\end{quote}

She stops recording. Almost adds an explanation. Doesn't.\par

Jo offers one end of the heavy case. Inez takes it. They carry it together toward the steps.\par

The empty bucket stays at the deep end.\par

\begin{flushright}FADE OUT.\end{flushright}

\begin{flushright}THE END\end{flushright}
