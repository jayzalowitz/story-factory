# Check representation with actual conversation evidence
Read QUALITY.md and the screenplay in scope. Apply the named-women variant: at least two named women talk to each other about something other than a man. Confirm character identity from the script or roster; do not infer gender from names, voices, or stereotypes. Quote a short exchange and identify who is addressing whom, its actual subject, and a stable scene ID. Two women in the same room, alternating monologues, or talking solely about a male lead do not establish a pass.

For a complete draft, give PASS, FAIL, or UNCLEAR with criterion-by-criterion evidence and coverage. A passing excerpt may establish a qualifying exchange but does not certify an unseen full film; a failing excerpt cannot prove a full-draft fail. If facts are unclear, say so. Keep broader agency/representation and writing quality separate from the binary test. Never add a token scene just to manipulate the result. Suggest a dramatically useful improvement only when it serves the story; preserve an intentionally constrained cast and report its consequences honestly.

Write development/bechdel_report.md and development/bechdel.json. JSON fields: draft_sha256 (from story_check.py fingerprint), coverage ('full-draft' or 'excerpt'), status ('PASS'|'FAIL'|'UNCLEAR'), summary, evidence (list of {scene_id,women:[names],exchange,non_man_topic,notes}), limitations. See the passing, failing, and uncertain examples in the toolkit examples/quality/README.md. This standalone check edits no screenplay unless asked. Complete no workflow stage merely because this check passed.
