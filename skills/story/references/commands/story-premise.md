# Challenge the movie we are choosing to make
Read the discovery artifacts, source-mode controls, and research report when applicable. Compare an intimate and an ambitious execution. Test the audience promise, the specific dramatic engine, the central relationship, title promise, ending hypothesis, protagonist agency, and production envelope. Choose expand/hold/reduce scope with reasons, not because a grander film sounds more impressive.

Write development/story_bible.md and development/premise_review.md: concrete central question, external want and contradiction, core characters with independent wants, thematic tensions, rules of time/viewpoint, ending action and its cost, what makes this story irreplaceable, top risks, and ways scenes could test them. Flag source-based uncertainties separately from invented choices. Review the concept against the writer's intended experience. Record a truthful verdict and specific remaining weaknesses. Complete the premise stage only after the research gate (if applicable) and discovery are valid. Use MASTER_PROMPT.md sections 1–2 for the full craft requirements.
