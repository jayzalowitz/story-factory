# Review the story, not the size of the output
Read QUALITY.md and the entire draft. Assess dramatic spine, protagonist agency, scene causality, escalation/pacing, distinct relationships and voices, subtext/action, supporting-character independence, emotional specificity and tonal range, form/chronology, ending/title payoff, production logic, and research fidelity where applicable. Cite the scenes that establish each judgment. Mark unavailable areas unassessed; do not invent numerical certainty or coverage.

Save development/quality_report.md with strong/revise/unassessed results by dimension, biggest issues, protected strengths, prioritized repairs, and limits. Save development/quality.json with draft_sha256 from the fingerprint command, coverage, verdict ('READY' or 'REVISE'), summary, findings, and accepted_risks. Only include user-accepted risks when the user actually accepted them. READY means no unresolved issue that defeats the requested draft's core design, not professional certification or guaranteed market success. A serious flaw can make the draft REVISE even when the Bechdel test passes. Run the Bechdel procedure separately if missing or stale. For source-based modes, run story-truth before final verification. Mechanical quality validation checks report currency and fields, not literary merit.

Optional parallel research/review follows TEAMS.md and development/teams.json, off by default. Keep one author and do not expose these settings unless useful or requested.
