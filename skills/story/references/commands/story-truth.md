# story-truth — Check the promise against the evidence

Use after research, before a real-story outline locks, after consequential revisions, and before pitches or public copy make factual claims. Claude example: `/story-truth`. Codex example: `$story-truth`. Use the installed host entrypoint's actual supported syntax.

> /story-truth Check the outline against our sources. Flag invented dialogue, combined people, changed dates, and any claim the pitch overstates.

Read `COMMON.md`, `RESEARCH.md`, `research/source_mode.json`, the brief and actual creative instructions, research ledger, scene map, current story, packaging, and workflow status. This command evaluates the user's intended mode; it must not quietly rewrite a based-on project into fiction to obtain a pass.

1. Confirm the mode and proposed audience-facing wording. For `original`, check applicable factual assertions and any accidental implication of a true biography; do not require evidence for invented action. For `based-on` and `inspired-by`, apply the conditional real-story gate in the workflow tracker.
2. Read the relevant material in full for the review's stated scope. Match scenes to stable claim IDs. Inspect decisive sources and exact locations rather than trusting worker summaries or the presence of URLs. Distinguish established events, uncertain dates, motive inference, reconstructed dialogue, disputed allegations, and invented plot.
3. Audit `claims.json`: every source ID resolves; every verified assertion has inspected supporting evidence; quotations match the source; disputes retain contrary evidence; inferences identify reasoning; invented events do not masquerade as verified facts. Confidence does not override status. Flag inaccessible evidence explicitly.
4. Audit chronology, relationships, period/context, and scene traceability. Pay special attention to compression that reverses cause and effect, composites that relocate blame, invented private scenes presented as documented, and an ending that changes what a real person did.
5. Read `creative_departures`, living-person entries, and `source_material.md`. Surface specific unresolved source-use, privacy, or serious-allegation questions. Historical distance changes the questions; it does not prove accuracy or source ownership. Record permissions only when evidence shows they exist. Do not assert legal clearance or impose a blanket legal checklist on ordinary fictional scenes.
6. Compare title cards, logline, synopsis, pitch copy, site copy, and disclaimers with the actual work. Recommend precise wording appropriate to the mode. A generic disclaimer cannot repair a misleading factual claim. Never publish changes as part of this review.
7. Write `research/truth_report.md` with scope, reviewed artifacts, evidence, scene-level findings, repairs, open questions, and `Verdict: PASS` or `Verdict: REVISE`. Update the ledger and traceability to reflect supported corrections; preserve substantial creative alternatives for the writer. Follow the actual workflow interface to reopen affected stages when facts or scenes change.

A PASS requires a supportable stated truth promise, visible creative departures, usable traceability, and no concealed consequential contradiction in the checked scope. A REVISE identifies concrete repairs or missing evidence. Neither verdict certifies legal safety, independent review, exhaustive research, artistic quality, or permission to publish. Report native reviewers only if they actually ran; otherwise describe this honestly as the current agent's review.

For the final full-draft audit also write research/truth.json with every binding returned by `python3 scripts/story_check.py truth-fingerprint`, plus verdict and summary. The bindings cover the screenplay, claim ledger, source mode (including audience wording and departures), research reports/traceability, audience-facing package/pitch/README copy, and local source artifacts. Register saved evidence outside sources/ with a project-relative path or local_path on its ledger source. Remote URLs are citations, not automatic live-content checks; preserve inspected snapshots or notes when available. Missing bindings in older receipts require an actual review before renewal; never mechanically relabel them current. Use verdict READY only for the reviewed truth promise; use REVISE for unresolved consequential claims. `story_check.py truth-current` checks receipt currency before final verification. A concept-stage truth review does not cover a later screenplay.

For a novel, memoir, article, play or other supplied non-screenplay source, read ADAPTATION.md and use story-adapt. Source fidelity, adaptation permission status and factual truth are separate. Recheck current source-to-scene mapping after substantive changes.
