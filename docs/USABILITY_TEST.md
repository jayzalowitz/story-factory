# Usability method

The user chose three simulated beginner journeys because real participants are unavailable. Current results and limitations are in [SIMULATED_USABILITY.md](SIMULATED_USABILITY.md). Do not describe these as a human study or claim human success rates.

A future real study could observe account choice, download, opening a folder, activation, a creative decision, source import, selected output, revision, PDF opening and session resumption. It is not a prerequisite the current user asked us to retain, and no recruitment is planned.
